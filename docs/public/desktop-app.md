# Desktop app (beta)

The Tedix app brings your Tedix organization to your Mac and lets your coding
tools pick up Tedix tasks on this computer. You set everything up with clicks;
no terminal is needed.

## Download

- [Download for Mac with Apple chip](https://downloads.tedix.dev/desktop/releases/0.1.33/Tedix-0.1.33-darwin-arm64.zip)
- [Download for Mac with Intel chip](https://downloads.tedix.dev/desktop/releases/0.1.33/Tedix-0.1.33-darwin-x64.zip)

Not sure which one? Open the Apple menu, choose **About This Mac**, and look
at **Chip**.

## Set it up

1. Open the downloaded file and move **Tedix** to your Applications folder.
2. Open Tedix and choose **Sign in**. Your browser opens; sign in with your
   Tedix account.
3. Choose the coding tools you use and sign each one in to Tedix.

The app updates itself. You can change everything later in **Settings**
(⌘,).

Tedix Cloud is open by invitation; see [Release status](./release-status.md).
