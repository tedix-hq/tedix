Accepting one of the three things `tedix learn profile` offers now starts it, whether you accept in Tedix OS, in chat or with `tedix learn review`: a task is handed to the tedi it names (or your default tedi), a factory is planned for you to switch on with `tedix factory create`, and a document is drafted in your space. The answer says what started and links to it, and accepting twice starts nothing twice. Workstreams keep their names and their place in the document across runs, so a re-run updates the same sections. "Your week" only names products, people, numbers and versions found in your sessions; anything else is left out.
