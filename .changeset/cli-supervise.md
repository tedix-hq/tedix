`tedix supervise` delivers a tedi auto reply that arrives after a Codex session's 5-minute `await-draft` wait: to an open Codex window through `codex queue`, or by resuming the session headless. `tedix supervise install` runs it at login as a macOS LaunchAgent; `tedix supervise uninstall` removes it.
