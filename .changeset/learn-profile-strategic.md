`tedix learn profile` opens with "Your week in Tedix" and groups your history into at most 12 workstreams, each with its threads as sub-bullets, weighted by the days you worked on them and what shipped rather than by message volume. A workstream is linked to a board project only on evidence (a Work Item of the project touched, its key or id written, or a repository path it names); otherwise it says "No board link". The 3 things it offers are strategic: a task handed to a tedi, a factory, or a drafted document, chosen by your goals and the stakes (customer, deadline, revenue, others blocked) and recency. Verify and commit chores stay in the open loops list. Proposals from an earlier run that the new run does not pick again are withdrawn from your inbox.
