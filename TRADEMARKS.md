# Tedix Trademark And Certification Policy

"Tedix", the Tedix logo, "Tedix Cloud", "tedi", and Tedix certification
badges are trademarks of Tedix GbR and its legal successors ("Tedix"). The
code licenses do not grant trademark rights.

## Allowed Without Permission

- **Truthful, nominative use:** "built on Tedix", "compatible with Tedix",
  "a plugin for Tedix", including in package names such as
  `tedix-plugin-<name>` that clearly indicate an independent work.
- **Unmodified redistribution:** distributing unmodified Tedix source or
  builds under their licenses, keeping the name and notices intact.
- **Commentary and comparison:** factual references in articles, talks,
  benchmarks, and documentation.

## Not Allowed Without Permission

- naming a modified fork, product, or hosted service "Tedix" or something
  confusingly similar, or using the Tedix logo for it
- implying endorsement, partnership, or official status
- claiming Tedix certification ("Tedix Certified", "certified for Tedix") or
  using Tedix certification badges outside the Tedix certification program
- registering domains, company names, or app-store listings whose primary
  identifier is a Tedix mark

## Forks

Forks are permitted by the licenses. A materially modified public fork must
use its own name and branding, may state "forked from Tedix" factually, and
must not ship Tedix certification badges.

## Certification Marks

Certification claims ("Tedix Certified", certified runtime badges) are granted only through the Tedix certification program and may be
revoked if certification lapses.
